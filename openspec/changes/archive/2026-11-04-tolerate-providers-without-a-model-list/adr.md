# ADR Review

## Reviewed

- ADR-0014 Prices are proposed, never fetched at runtime
- ADR-0022 Free-pool membership may be updated on a schedule without review
- ADR-0023 Answers that describe the endpoint make it unavailable

## Outcome

No major durable architectural decision was introduced. This refines the exit codes and report of a command added by an earlier change; discovery's behavior and the command-only boundary (ADR 0022) are unchanged.

## New ADRs

None.
